WHO WE ARE

Your expert in
Specialty fertilizers

DeltaChem is a leading manufacturer of specialty fertilizers designed to support modern agriculture and horticulture across the globe. With production facilities in the Netherlands, Spain, and Germany, and a dedicated sales office in Chile, we serve growers worldwide with innovative and efficient fertilizing solutions.

Our portfolio includes advanced products such as controlled release fertilizers (CRFs), stabilized nitrogen fertilizers, and high-performance biostimulants, developed in our in-house laboratories and R&D centers.
